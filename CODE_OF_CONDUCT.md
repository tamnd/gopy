# Code of Conduct

The `gopy` project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) version
2.1.

## Our standards

We expect everyone interacting in the project (issues, pull requests,
discussions, code review, social channels) to be respectful,
constructive, and patient. Concretely:

* Use welcoming and inclusive language.
* Disagree with ideas, not with people.
* Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Publishing private information without explicit permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or events.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with
it, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub
by opening a confidential security advisory at
<https://github.com/tamnd/gopy/security/advisories/new> and mark the
report as a code-of-conduct issue. Reports are handled confidentially.
